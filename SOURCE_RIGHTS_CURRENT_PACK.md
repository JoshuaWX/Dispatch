# Dispatch current rights-cleared evidence-pack check (24 September 2026)

**Verdict: fail for autonomous publication; do not start the publishing soak.** No reviewed no-fee, first-party *same-topic* pack was verified that simultaneously proves four distinct article-level pages, three independent publisher domains, two original publication dates in the preceding 72 hours, and a material claim independently corroborated by two publishers. This is a sampling result, not a claim that no such pack can ever exist. The strongest tested case below is the 2026 Arctic sea-ice minimum. [NASA report](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/earth-science/climate-science/sea-ice/arctic-sea-ice-2026-min/); [NSIDC release](https://nsidc.org/news-analyses/news-stories/arctic-sea-ice-has-reached-minimum-extent-2026-antarctic-sea-ice-maximum-most-likely-reached-well); [NSIDC analysis](https://nsidc.org/ru/node/431985).

## Gate decision and next evidence required

**Counted as proven:** at least two fresh, article-level first-party pages; high-reliability source; plausible no-fee text permissions with qualifications. **Not proven:** a four-page package that is both same-story and rights-cleared, three genuinely independent publishers, independent corroboration of a material current-event claim, page-level third-party exclusions, and an automated fetching policy for every candidate. [NASA report](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/earth-science/climate-science/sea-ice/arctic-sea-ice-2026-min/); [NSIDC release](https://nsidc.org/news-analyses/news-stories/arctic-sea-ice-has-reached-minimum-extent-2026-antarctic-sea-ice-maximum-most-likely-reached-well); [NSIDC citation policy](https://nsidc.org/ru/node/46234); [ECMWF terms](https://www.ecmwf.int/en/terms-use).

Keep NewsAPI free/unsure and Virlo unapproved disabled as already decided. Sample first-party feeds in staging **without contacting Gemini or publishing**. Store original publication timestamp separately from updates; record author, publisher organisation, article-level rights notice, licence attribution, excluded third-party blocks, retrieval policy, and whether a corroborating publisher used the same underlying release/dataset. Only a genuinely qualifying current pack should permit the Gemini publication soak; otherwise continue paused or obtain explicit source licences. The code's [domain-based corroboration rule](frontend/lib/pipeline.ts) is not sufficient by itself to detect a shared upstream announcement.
