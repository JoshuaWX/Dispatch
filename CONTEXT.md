# Dispatch editorial context

Dispatch publishes AI-written news only when a bounded evidence package supports every material claim. A scheduled attempt may end without a publication.

## Language

**Official announcement**:
A newsworthy statement or release issued by the organisation directly responsible for the announced action or finding. One current, rights-cleared authoritative primary source may support a brief confined to what that organisation says.
_Avoid_: Independently confirmed story

**Developing story**:
An account that combines reporting or interpretation beyond one issuer's announcement. It needs corroboration from at least two independent source organisations and two independent upstream origins.
_Avoid_: Multi-URL story

**Source organisation**:
The accountable publisher or institution that produced evidence, not a hostname, mirror, syndication outlet, or page count.
_Avoid_: Domain

**Upstream origin**:
The underlying release, study, dataset, observation, or independently produced report from which a source's material claims derive. Pages repeating one origin are one line of evidence.
_Avoid_: URL

**Rights-cleared evidence**:
A retrieved article-level page whose own displayed terms explicitly allow the limited reuse required for evidence-based drafting, with the licence and attribution retained.
_Avoid_: Search hit, snippet, domain allowlist

**Publication attempt**:
One scheduled or operator-triggered evaluation of at most one candidate; a safe skip is a valid outcome.
_Avoid_: Publication target
